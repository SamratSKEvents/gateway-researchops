# Purpose

ResearchOps helps people make better business decisions by turning open-ended research questions into clear, evidence-backed reports. It is designed for questions about markets, products, competitors, customers, and other business decisions where useful information is spread across many sources.

ResearchOps should make research easier to verify. It breaks a question into focused tasks, gathers information from multiple sources, records claims with supporting quotations and source details, and compares the evidence before drawing conclusions. Its report explains what the evidence suggests, where sources disagree, what remains unknown, and which findings could change the decision.

## The problem

Business research often requires people to search many websites and reports, decide what is relevant, compare claims, check whether information is current, and assemble the findings into a useful report. Important evidence can be missed, repeated claims can look like independent confirmation, and conflicting or outdated information can be hidden in a summary.

## What ResearchOps is for

ResearchOps supports decision-makers such as founders, analysts, product managers, consultants, and strategy teams. For each research question, it aims to:

- Clarify the decision and scope before research begins.
- Break the question into focused, well-defined research tasks.
- Gather and compare evidence from more than one source.
- Keep important claims traceable to source links, dates, and supporting quotations.
- Surface duplicate, conflicting, stale, and unverified information.
- Produce a structured report with findings, opportunities, risks, limitations, and a clear account of uncertainty.

ResearchOps is decision support. Its conclusions should show how they follow from the collected evidence and make assumptions and gaps visible, so a person can review the findings and decide what to do.

## Hackathon demonstration

The demonstration should show one complete live run: start with an open-ended business research question, show how ResearchOps divides it into tasks, gather and compare findings across sources, then present a clear final report with supporting sources that viewers can check.
